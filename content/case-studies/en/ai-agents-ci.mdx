## TL;DR
I implemented an **issue → plan → exec → PR → review → patch** flow using Forgejo bots, dedicated runners, and human approval. The goal is to speed up repetitive work without losing traceability or quality: everything goes through issues, PRs, reviews, and guardrails.

![Agent workflow overview](/media/case-studies/ai-agents-ci/diagram-0-overview.svg)

## Role & Scope
**Role:** DevOps / Platform (owner)  
**Period:** 2024–present  
**Scope:** workflow design, runner separation by permissions, guardrails, operation, and continuous improvement.

## Profile relevance
- Primary: DevOps / Platform (CI/CD automation, platform ops)
- Secondary: Configuration Management (process discipline and change control)

## Context
I wanted a development flow where AI is **not magic**, but a tool inside the process: clear planning, controlled implementation, and review before merge. Forgejo provides the right building blocks (issues + PRs + workflows), so I integrated LLM agents as participants in the pipeline.

## Deep dives

### Deep dive — Workflow architecture (Forgejo + runners)
- **Forgejo** as the hub (issues, PRs, reviews).
- **Dedicated runners**:
  - Implementer bot: broader permissions for build/run.
  - Reviewer bot: reduced permissions for analysis and feedback.
- **LLM integration** via containerized workflows.

![Architecture](/media/case-studies/ai-agents-ci/diagram-1-architecture.svg)

### Deep dive — Command flow (plan → exec → review → patch)
- `run/plan` → produces plan, risks, and questions.
- `run/exec` → implements on a branch, opens PR.
- `run/review` → automated review.
- `run/patch` → iterates fixes on the same branch.

![Workflow steps](/media/case-studies/ai-agents-ci/diagram-2-workflow.svg)

### Deep dive — Guardrails + human control
- **Spec-first:** issues with clear goals.
- **PR review required** (human or reviewer bot).
- **Iteration and cost limits** (still improving).
- **Human final decision** to merge.

![Guardrails](/media/case-studies/ai-agents-ci/diagram-3-guardrails.svg)

**AI-assisted disclaimer (honest):**
- I define objectives, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
- The AI implements based on those specs.
- I validate end-to-end and review critical changes.
- Everything goes through PRs, review, and rollback if needed.

## Outcomes & Impact
- The workflow is live and used for real tasks.
- Reduces time for repetitive changes with full traceability.
- **Current limitation:** a bug in the iteration counter can cause loops and unexpected cost (identified).
- **Next steps:** tighter cost guardrails, runner hardening, anonymized visual evidence (issues/PRs).

![Cost loop](/media/case-studies/ai-agents-ci/diagram-4-cost-loop.svg)

## Learnings
- The best place for AI is **inside the human workflow** (issues + PRs).
- Without guardrails, AI can burn budget quickly.
- Permission-separated runners reduce risk.
