## TL;DR
During my Airbus internship I worked on **data analytics for engineering decision‑making**: estimating change impact (especially **weight reduction**) from **product structures (BOMs)** of the **A320** (HTP/VTP components) filtered by **Manufacturing Serial Number (MSN)** or by **applicability/configuration**. The outcome was a **dashboard** (pilot‑ready) supported by a **pipeline** (PySpark/SQL) to query product structure, count standard parts, and compute **total weight** and **before/after deltas** when substituting standards.

## Context
In engineering/design, for improvement campaigns (e.g., weight reduction) or maintenance decisions, it’s critical to answer questions quickly:
- “If I replace this standard with a lighter one, how many instances does it affect by applicability/MSN?”
- “What total weight does this standard represent within HTP/VTP for a given configuration?”
- “Where in the structure does it appear and how many times?”

**Project objective:** build a data product (pipeline + dashboard) that enables **trade‑off analysis**:
1. Filter by **MSN** or **applicability/configuration**.
2. Extract the **HTP/VTP BOM** and navigate levels.
3. Count instances of a given **standard part**.
4. Compute **total weight** and **before/after** comparisons for substitutions.

## Deep dives

### Deep dive — Data sources & model (no sensitive details)
- Data came from an internal **legacy PLM** (A320 historical).
- Airbus generated a **database bank** recreated from that legacy system.
- The project used:
  - **physical product structures (BOMs)** for HTP/VTP,
  - parent‑child relationships to navigate assembly → subassembly → part → standard part,
  - applicability data (MSN/configuration).

**Enrichment: standards catalog**
- A **standard parts** table provided material, unit weight, and metadata.

![BOM drill-down](/media/case-studies/airbus-data/diagram-2-bom-drilldown.svg)

### Deep dive — Pipeline + dashboard
**Pipeline (transforms / joins)**
- Block‑style pipelines with **PySpark/SQL** transforms.
- Filtering by **MSN** or **applicability/configuration**.
- Reconstruction of the **HTP/VTP product structure**.
- Aggregations and joins with the standards catalog (weight/material).
- Publishing datasets ready for the dashboard.

![Pipeline blocks](/media/case-studies/airbus-data/diagram-4-pipeline-blocks.svg)

**Dashboard (user-facing)**
- Select **MSN** or **applicability/configuration**.
- View **BOM** with level navigation.
- Search for **standard parts** by code.
- Compute counts, total weight, and **before/after** deltas.

![User flow](/media/case-studies/airbus-data/diagram-3-user-flow.svg)

### Deep dive — Validation & “missing data” detection
**Situation:** reconstructing the **HTP/VTP BOM** from the recreated database.

**Task:** validate that the structure matched the legacy reference.

**Action:**
- Exported the BOM by MSN from the recreated DB.
- Exported the equivalent structure from the legacy tool.
- Diffed both CSVs with a script and produced a **differences report**.
- Escalated to the DB‑bank owners with repro steps and scripts.

**Result:**
- Total BOM ~**1000** items.
- **~10–15** missing instances, concentrated in a specific standard type.
- Real risk of bias in counts and total weight.
