## TL;DR
I worked on the **Eurodrone** program supporting day‑to‑day **configuration management**: ensuring traceability, preparing change documentation, and coordinating across multiple stakeholders. This was my operational CM foundation before acting as the “customer” in One Platform.

## Role & Scope
**Role:** Configuration Management Analyst (ALTEN, embedded at Airbus)  
**Location:** Getafe (Madrid)  
**Period:** 08/2023 – 09/2025  
**Scope:** program CM support, change readiness, artifact preparation, coordination with internal and external teams.

## Profile relevance
- Primary: Configuration Management (change control, traceability, readiness)
- Secondary: Systems Engineering (stakeholder alignment + baselines)

## Context
Eurodrone is a high‑rigor aerospace program with a demanding change process. In that environment, CM is not just documentation—it is the mechanism that preserves coherence, traceability, and compliance across the lifecycle.
