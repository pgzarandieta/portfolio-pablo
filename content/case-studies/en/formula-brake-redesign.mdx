## TL;DR
After a critical failure during technical inspection, I redesigned the brake system to make it **robust, adjustable, and manufacturable**. The new design passed inspection and subsequent validation, reinforcing a design‑for‑manufacture mindset.

## Role & Scope
**Role:** Mechanical Design + Manufacturing  
**Period:** 2022  
**Scope:** CAD design, interference review, tolerances, coordination with manufacturing, and validation.

## Profile relevance
- Primary: Mechanical Engineering (design, manufacturing, validation)
- Secondary: Configuration Management (change control + revision traceability)

## Context
During Formula Student technical inspection, a “hard press” on the brake caused a **critical failure**. The system had to be redesigned quickly without compromising performance or safety.

## Deep dives

### Deep dive — Design for manufacture
- Geometry adjustments for **3‑axis machining**.
- CAD interference checks.
- Realistic tolerances with machinist feedback.

### Deep dive — Integration & validation
- Coordination with chassis and allowed mounting points.
- Selection of bushings / friction elements.
- Verification in post‑assembly tests.

## Outcomes & Impact
- Reinforced brake system that **passed inspection** and later validation.
- Better understanding of **design for manufacture** and real tolerances.

## Learnings
- Manufacturability is as critical as performance.
- Field validation reveals risks CAD alone may miss.
