## TL;DR
I built a **self-hosted homelab** on Fedora Server with **Podman/Podman-Compose**, **Nginx reverse proxy**, **Tailscale** for secure access, and **Authentik** as the identity provider (SSO). On top of that, I deployed real‑world suites (family/partner use) and productivity: Nextcloud/Collabora, Forgejo + runners + registry, Firefly III + Metabase, and a custom app (Finance ETL Console). I also integrated **LLM agents in Forgejo** (plan/exec/review/patch) to automate PRs with human guardrails.

## Context
I wanted my own infrastructure (privacy + control) with secure remote access, SSO and permissions, and self‑hosted apps with product thinking (docs, reproducibility, roadmap). I also wanted to automate development with CI/CD and integrated agents.

## Deep dives

### Deep dive — Platform infra (runtime + proxy + access)
**What I implemented**
- Fedora Server + Podman/Compose runtime.
- Nginx reverse proxy with TLS (Tailscale certs).
- Subpath routing, local DNS with Pi-hole.
- `infra/` + `data/` structure separating stacks and persistence.

**Tradeoffs / decisions**
- Subpaths vs subdomains (less initial friction, more complexity for some apps).
- Partial autostart (systemd/quadlets pending).

### Deep dive — Identity & access (SSO)
- Authentik as the central IdP.
- OIDC/SAML/Forward Auth depending on the app.
- Goal: reduce friction and improve traceability per user/group.

### Deep dive — AI agents in CI (Dev workflow)
[Go to standalone case study: AI agents in CI →](/projects/ai-agents-ci)

**How it works**
- Forgejo bots (`sabaris-ai-bot`, `sabaris-review-bot`).
- Issue commands: `run/plan`, `run/exec`, `run/review`, `run/patch`.
- Dedicated runners: implementer vs reviewer (least‑privilege).

**Results & limits**
- Faster iterations with human final control.
- **Current gap:** iteration counter bug → cost risk.

- Operational self‑hosted platform with multi‑user SSO.

## Learnings
- **SSO is a game‑changer** for multi‑user adoption.
- **Agents + PRs** fit human workflows well.
- Publishing infra is a project in itself (sanitized, documented, reproducible).
